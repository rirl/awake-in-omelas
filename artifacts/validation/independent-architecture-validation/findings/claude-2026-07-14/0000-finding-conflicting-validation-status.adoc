= Finding: Conflicting Breakpoint Beta Validation Status
:toc:

== Summary

Three documents describe the same milestone — end-to-end operation of
Open WebUI → MCPO → Filesystem MCP → Governed Repository — and give three
different answers about whether it is done.

== Evidence

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Document |Status Claimed |Key Quote

|`docs/architecture/todo-federated-ai-platform.adoc`
(last touched 2026-07-02)
|Not started
|Phase 2 checklist entirely unchecked: `[ ] Validate repository browsing`,
`[ ] Validate repository search`, `[ ] Validate document retrieval`.

|`docs/operations/openwebui-bootstrap-notes.adoc`
(2026-07-03)
|Done
|"This validation demonstrates successful end-to-end repository-backed
reasoning." Status fields throughout read "Validated."

|`artifacts/infrastructure/breakpoint-beta/README.adoc` +
`artifacts/infrastructure/breakpoint-beta/notes.adoc`
(2026-07-14, same day as this checkpoint)
|Pending
|"Status: Research Prototype" / "Disposition: Archived as reference
implementation" / "Superseded By: The first validated end-to-end
Breakpoint Beta implementation (pending)."
|===

== Why It Matters

None of the three documents references the other two. A reader who
consults only one would form a materially different picture of project
status than a reader who consults another. No mechanism in the repository
(index, cross-link, validation record) currently surfaces the
contradiction.

== Likely Reconciliation

`openwebui-bootstrap-notes.adoc`'s "Validated" most plausibly describes a
manual, ad hoc proof-of-concept (`curl`, `pgrep`, one interactive Open
WebUI session) — real evidence the pieces *can* connect, but not a
repeatable or governed validation. `breakpoint-beta/README.adoc`, written
later, appears to take the more considered position: that
proof-of-concept does not constitute the accepted Breakpoint Beta
implementation, hence its disposition as an archived research artifact
rather than a merged result.

== Recommendation

. Update `openwebui-bootstrap-notes.adoc` to qualify its "Validated"
claim as exploratory/manual, with an explicit pointer to the disposition
recorded in `breakpoint-beta/README.adoc`.
. Update the Phase 2 checklist in `todo-federated-ai-platform.adoc` to
reflect that manual exploration has occurred, distinct from governed,
repeatable validation.
. Do not checkpoint Breakpoint Beta as complete until all three documents
agree on a single status.
