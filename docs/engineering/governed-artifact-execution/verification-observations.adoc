== GAE-0006 Context-Preservation Observation

identified from reviewer AsciiDoc knowledge rather than from an existing
repository-local validation rule.

Inspection of `scripts/engineering/detect-adoc-section-spacing.bash` confirmed
that the current detector checks spacing before section headings only. It does
not check paragraph-to-list transitions.

The candidate was manually corrected before admission by inserting the missing
blank line between the introductory paragraph and the list.

This observation is preserved as a provisional candidate for future validation
tooling. GAE-0006 does not establish that such a rule should be implemented,
canonized, or admitted. The candidate remains subject to further empirical
verification and triage.
