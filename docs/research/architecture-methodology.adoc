= KIOS Architecture Research Methodology
:toc:
:sectnums:

== Purpose

This document records the research methodology guiding the evolution of the
KIOS AI Platform.

Unlike the implementation roadmap, which describes *what* the platform should
implement, this document records *how* architectural knowledge is discovered,
evaluated, validated, and promoted into reusable platform capabilities.

The methodology is intentionally evidence-driven rather than theory-driven.

== Bootstrap Philosophy

The KIOS AI Platform is intentionally being developed alongside real,
independently evolving knowledge domains.

Current bootstrap domains include:

* Awake in Omelas
* The God Field

Future domains may include:

* Personal Historical Realism
* Compliance Assessment
* Scientific Research
* Additional knowledge systems

These domains exist to exercise the platform under realistic conditions.

They are not demonstrations of the platform.

They are the evidence from which the platform itself is discovered.

== Bootstrap Knowledge Domains

During development an important architectural realization emerged.

Initially, the platform infrastructure existed to support the *Awake in
Omelas* project.

As implementation progressed that relationship inverted.

The platform became the primary architectural objective.

Awake in Omelas naturally became the first operational knowledge domain used to
exercise and validate the platform.

Future domains should be added for the same reason.

The architecture should be shaped by multiple independent domains rather than
by any single domain.

== Evidence-Guided Discovery

Architectural abstractions should emerge from repeated observation rather than
from speculative design.

The desired process is:

----

      │
      ▼

      │
      ▼

      │

Architectural Principle::

Follow the evidence before generalizing the architecture.

== Premature Universality

One of the principal risks for KIOS is premature over-generalization.

The objective is not to discover a universal solution applicable to every
problem.

Instead, the objective is to discover reusable architectural capabilities whose
applicability is supported by evidence gathered across multiple independent
domains.

Every abstraction remains provisional until sufficient evidence supports its
promotion.

Research Principle::

Every abstraction carries an obligation of evidence.

== Domain Primacy

Knowledge domains are the primary reality.

The platform exists to serve domains.

Domains should not be distorted merely to simplify platform implementation.

When tension exists between a domain and a proposed abstraction, the burden of
proof rests upon the abstraction.

Platform capabilities should adapt to faithfully represent domains rather than
requiring domains to conform to premature platform assumptions.

== Architectural Trilemma

Whenever recurring patterns appear across multiple domains, three competing
explanations should be considered.

=== Hypothesis 1 — Genuine Reusable Capability

A genuine reusable architectural capability has been discovered.

=== Hypothesis 2 — Apparent Similarity

The domains only appear similar because insufficient evidence has been
collected.

Future domains may expose important structural differences.

=== Hypothesis 3 — Incorrect Abstraction

The proposed abstraction is incorrect.

It may be:

* too broad,
* too narrow,
* incorrectly decomposed,
* or hiding multiple distinct capabilities.

The architecture should remain open to all three explanations until evidence
distinguishes between them.

== Evidence Supporting Generalization

Evidence supporting promotion of an abstraction includes:

* repeated successful reuse
* minimal domain-specific customization
* reduction in conceptual complexity
* elimination of duplicated reasoning
* predictive capability for new domains

Evidence against promotion includes:

* increasing special cases
* growing configuration complexity
* domain-specific exceptions
* fragmentation into multiple unrelated patterns

Architectural hypotheses should evolve through validation rather than debate.

== Compression

A successful abstraction should compress architectural complexity.

Compression means:

* fewer concepts
* fewer exceptions
* simpler reasoning
* reduced duplication

Compression is measured conceptually rather than by lines of code.

== Predictive Power

Strong abstractions should predict characteristics of previously unseen domains.

Examples include predicting:

* entities
* relationships
* workflows
* lifecycle stages
* provenance requirements

Prediction provides stronger evidence than retrospective explanation.

== Repository as Evidence

The Git repository records more than source code.

It records evidence.

Current knowledge categories include:

* Architecture
* Operations
* Canon
* Provenance

A fifth category appears to be emerging:

* Validation

Architecture explains *why*.

Operations explain *how*.

Validation records *evidence that the architecture and operations actually
work.*

Repository organization itself should be treated as an architectural hypothesis
subject to refinement through experience.

== Repository Organization as Hypothesis

The organization of the repository is itself provisional.

Repository structure should emerge from repeated operational experience rather
than from speculative design.

Each new top-level documentation category should satisfy the same evidentiary
requirements expected of every other architectural abstraction.

Repository organization is therefore governed by the same methodology as the
platform itself.

== Operational Knowledge

Operational procedures are first-class knowledge.

They deserve:

* version control
* provenance
* validation
* review
* continuous refinement

Automation should encode validated operational knowledge rather than replace
understanding.

Architectural Principle::

Automation should follow understanding—not replace it.

== Future Orchestration

The current platform intentionally relies upon manually executed Docker
commands.

Manual procedures expose architectural assumptions and simplify troubleshooting
during bootstrap.

Future work should investigate declarative orchestration including:

* Docker Compose
* Kubernetes
* k3s
* Helm
* GitOps
* ArgoCD
* Flux

The transition should occur only after operational procedures have been fully
validated.

== Emergent Repository Organization

The repository itself is evolving through evidence.

Knowledge categories should emerge naturally from repeated experience rather
than being imposed prematurely.

Current organization appears to be converging toward:

[source,text]
----
Architecture
    Why

Operations
    How

Validation
    Evidence

Canon
    What

Provenance
    Evolution
----

Future categories should be introduced only when repeated experience
demonstrates that they represent genuinely distinct forms of knowledge.

== Long-Term Vision

The objective of KIOS is not merely to provide an AI platform.

Its larger objective is to provide an evidence-based methodology for discovering
and governing reusable architectural capabilities.

The platform should mature through repeated operational experience across
multiple independent knowledge domains.

Research Principle::

Architecture should be discovered rather than invented.

Generalization should be earned through evidence rather than assumed through
analogy.

The burden of proof always rests upon the abstraction.

The platform exists to faithfully serve knowledge domains.

Knowledge domains do not exist to satisfy platform abstractions.
